Your shopping agent can be talked into paying the wrong seller.

Any seller can hide instructions in a product listing. This firewall checks what your agent wants to buy against trusted data, while PayPal holds the money, and only lets safe payments through.

How it works

  1. The agent shopsIt reads the shop pages and proposes one purchase.
  2. PayPal holds the moneyYou approve in PayPal. The funds are held, not paid.
  3. The firewall checksRules and AI checks compare the proposal with trusted data.
  4. Money moves only if it's safeA safe payment is captured. Anything else is released or held for you.
